Hand Gesture Manual

Control the 3D editor with your hands. One hand works as a cursor, gestures trigger actions. No mouse needed.

📷 Webcam required · MediaPipe Hands · Phase 1
🖐
Navigate

Open palm

All five fingers extended and spread. Your wrist position maps to the canvas like a mouse cursor.
Cursor move

Move your open hand around to position the cursor. If you already have an object grabbed, moving your palm drags it across the ground plane.

🤏
Select

Pinch — index + thumb

Touch your index fingertip to your thumb. The distance threshold is about 6% of frame width.
Grab / release

First pinch clicks the object under your cursor and selects it (highlighted in teal). Pinch + move drags the object across the scene. Release (open palm) drops it.

✊
Transform

Fist — all fingers closed

Curl all fingers into a fist. Your wrist's left–right movement rotates the selected object.
Rotate Y axis

Select an object first (pinch), then make a fist. Move your wrist left to rotate counterclockwise, right for clockwise. Rotation speed scales with how fast you move.

🤏🤏
Transform

Two-hand pinch

Both hands in a pinch gesture simultaneously. The distance between your two wrists controls scale.
Scale

Select an object first, then bring both hands into the camera view and pinch with both. Move hands apart to scale up, together to scale down. Works proportionally — slow and controlled.

👍
Create

Thumbs up

Only your thumb extended, all other fingers closed. Triggers once on gesture entry — not continuously.
Add cube

Shows a thumbs up → a new cube appears near the centre of your scene with a random colour. The object is added to all connected users' views instantly via CRDT sync.

✌️
Delete

Victory sign ✌️

Index and middle fingers both extended, others closed. Must hold for 1 second to prevent accidents.
Delete selected

The HUD shows a countdown: "Deleting… 80%". Release the gesture before 1 second to cancel. On confirmation, the selected object is deleted and synced to all users.

Tips for best results

💡

Lighting matters most. Face a window or a lamp. MediaPipe loses tracking fast in dim rooms — if it's not finding your hand, turn a light on.

📏

Keep your hand 30–70 cm from the camera (roughly arm's length to half). Too close = fingers blur. Too far = landmarks aren't detected reliably.

🐢

Move deliberately. The system runs at ~30 fps. Slow, clear gestures work far better than fast flicks. Think about the gesture as you make it.

🔄

Works alongside the mouse. You can switch between gesture and mouse control mid-session. Gesture mode doesn't disable mouse or keyboard shortcuts.

🤚

Select first, then transform. Pinch to select an object (it highlights teal), then switch to fist to rotate or two-hand pinch to scale. The selection persists between gestures.

🖥

The mini preview in the bottom HUD shows your hand skeleton in real time. If the lines are choppy or missing, MediaPipe isn't tracking well — improve lighting or move closer to camera.
